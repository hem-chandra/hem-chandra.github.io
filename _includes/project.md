## Synthetic Data Generation – Responsible AI Theme
**Role:** Research Team Member  
**Organization:** IndiaAI–IBD, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India  
**Duration:** Feb 2025 – Present  

Contributing to the development of AI tools and frameworks to enhance fairness in machine learning by generating synthetic data and mitigating biased labels.  

**Publications from this project:**  
- <b>H. C. Joshi</b> and S. Kumar, “FairGenerate: Enhancing fairness through synthetic data generation and two-fold biased labels removal,” *ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology*, Apr. 2025.  
- <b>H. C. Joshi</b> and S. Kumar, “FairPreprocessor: Better Fairness via Addressing Imbalanced Data through Synthetic Data Generation and Mitigating Biased Labels,” *IEEE Intelligent Systems*, doi: 10.1109/MIS.2025.3580459.

## Understanding People’s Attitude Towards Food in India – VAJRA Scheme
**Role:** Researcher  
**Organization:** IIT Roorkee & University of South Carolina (VAJRA Scheme, DST, Government of India)  
**Duration:** Dec 2021 - Oct 2022

Collaborated with Prof. Sandeep Kumar (IIT Roorkee) and Prof. Biplav Srivastava (University of South Carolina) to study people’s attitudes toward food in India and its implications for designing **fair AI systems**.  

**Publication from this project:**  
- <b>H. C. Joshi</b>, U. Yadav, B. Srivastava, and R. M. Singh, “Learning About People’s Attitude Towards Food Available in India and Its Implications for Fair AI-based Systems,” *2022 IEEE International Conference on Data Mining Workshops (ICDMW)*, Orlando, FL, USA, 2022, pp. 985–992, doi: 10.1109/ICDMW58026.2022.00128.
